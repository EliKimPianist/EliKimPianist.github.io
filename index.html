Research

Current Research

Reexamining the Toccata: From Improvisation to Compositional Control

A performer-centered study of the keyboard toccata, tracing its transformation from seventeenth-century improvisatory practice to more compositionally controlled twentieth-century works.

Research Interests

  • Keyboard toccata
  • Improvisation & performance practice
  • Girolamo Frescobaldi
  • Seventeenth-century keyboard music
  • Twentieth-century piano repertoire

Related Projects
